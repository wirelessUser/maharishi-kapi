Meet the Founder

Acharya Alok

Vedic Astrologer · Vastu Expert · Founder, Maharishi Kapi Institute

Acharya Alok, known to thousands of seekers simply as Guruji, is a ninth-generation Vedic astrologer and lifelong student of dharma.

+

He carries a nine-generation legacy

Raised in a family that has practiced Jyotish, Vastu, and Ayurveda for over two centuries, Acharya Alok inherited his craft not from a classroom but from a lineage of gurus. He formalized this knowledge with advanced study in Vedic sciences before dedicating his life to sharing it with the world.

After witnessing seekers lost to modern uncertainty

Acharya Alok decided to step beyond his hometown practice and reach people online. What began as a small satsang has grown into the Maharishi Kapi Institute, now serving thousands across India and abroad through consultations, courses, and community retreats rooted in authentic Vedic tradition.

His ability to read the cosmic blueprint

has helped guide decisions on marriage, career, and health for families across three generations. Acharya Alok's approach combines classical Jyotish charts with practical, compassionate counsel — never fear, always clarity.

He is now applying this ancient science

to modern life through the Institute's courses and consultations. He is the author of Destiny & Dharma, a practical guide to living in harmony with one's cosmic design, and continues to train the next generation of Acharyas through the Institute's certification programs.
